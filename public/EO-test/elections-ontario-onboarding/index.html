What to Expect on Day 1

Monday, May 5th. Here's how your first day will unfold.

9:00 AM
Arrival & Welcome Sarah will meet you at the 3rd floor reception. You'll get your access badge and a quick building tour.
9:30 AM
Laptop & Equipment Pickup Head to IT on the 2nd floor to collect your laptop, headset, and login credentials.
10:00 AM
Group Orientation Session Join fellow interns for a 90-minute orientation covering EO's mission, structure, and your role within it.
12:00 PM
Welcome Kit You'll receive your Elections Ontario welcome kit, including your Day One collectible pin!
1:00 PM
Meet the Team Informal team lunch with the Policy & Research group. A great chance to put faces to names.
2:30 PM
1-on-1 with Your Mentor Sit down with Sarah to go over your goals, schedule, and any questions you have heading into week one.
4:00 PM
Wrap-Up & Q&A End the day with a brief check-in. Ask anything, share your first impressions, and get set for day two.

Getting Here & Finding Your Way

51 Rolark Drive, Scarborough, ON M1R 3B1

Address: 51 Rolark Drive, Scarborough, ON M1R 3B1  ·  Nearest TTC: Bus stop at Midland & Rolark  ·  Parking: Free visitor lot on site (enter from Rolark Dr)
Security desk is immediately to your right as you enter the main entrance. Present your ID on Day 1. Your badge will be ready for pickup here. The main reception and visitor check-in are at the front desk. Elevators are straight ahead past reception.
The 2nd floor houses Electoral Operations and IT Services. The Orientation Room (Room 210) is where your Day 1 session will take place. It's the large boardroom directly off the elevator lobby on your left. There's a kitchenette and lounge area at the south end of the floor.
Your day-to-day workspace. The Policy & Research team occupies the north wing. Your assigned desk is in the open area near the windows. Sarah Leclair will show you on Day 1. The printer/supply room is Room 312. The rooftop terrace is accessible via the stairwell at the east end.
