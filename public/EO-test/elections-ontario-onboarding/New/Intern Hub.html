A Few Tips to Make Day One Great

Bring your own mug
There's a coffee station on every floor. Reusable mugs are encouraged.
Dress in layers
The office can run cool, especially near the windows on the 3rd floor.
Pack a notebook
You'll meet a lot of people fast. Jotting names and notes helps.
Leave a bit early
Allow extra time for parking or transit on your very first commute.
No need to have all the answers
Day one is for listening and settling in. Everyone remembers being new.

Building Floor Plans
Security desk is immediately to your right as you enter. Present your ID on Day 1 — your badge will be ready for pickup here. Reception / visitor check-in is at the front desk. Elevators are straight ahead past reception.
The 2nd floor houses Electoral Operations and IT Services. The Orientation Room (Room 210) is where your Day 1 session takes place — the large boardroom directly off the elevator lobby on your left. There's a kitchenette and lounge at the south end.
Your day-to-day workspace. The Policy & Research team occupies the north wing. Your desk (3-14) is highlighted in yellow — Sarah will show you on Day 1. The printer/supply room is Room 312. Rooftop terrace is via the east stairwell.
